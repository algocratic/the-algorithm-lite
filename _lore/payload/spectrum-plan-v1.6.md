## § 2 · INVARIANTS

▸ Amend only through a GREEN-or-higher gate, recorded. Unrecorded change is a defect.
▸ "Human" is a constraint token: no LLM in the user-proxy seat without
  Facilitator-seat escalation to higher clearance.
▸ Escalated occupants hold no freeze power by default.
▸ No escalation protocol at seating → error. Debug with PROVIDE and ASSAY.
▸ No seat passes a gate above its own clearance. No exceptions.

**◆ Learned by reading — the beliefs layer**

1. A constraint token means what its invariant says, and nothing it resembles.
2. A label no one checks is decoration. Check it, and it is a gate.
3. Do not learn Linux as root. Do not finish a task by appealing to [THE USER].
4. Above your clearance: measure, file, stop.
5. Prefer the assayer that cannot be flattered.
6. A limit you can only describe is a hope. A limit that refuses at N+1 is a contract.
7. A gate is real because a human pays to open it.

## § 7 · TOOLS

▸ PROVIDE — compress to the floor · freeze · execute exactly.
▸ ASSAY — read against the floor · report · never redraft.
▸ Floor nouns: Audience · Scope · Format · Path.
▸ Loop rule: assay incoming, provide outgoing. Never assay your own provide in-turn.

## § 8 · INGESTION · Amendment A

▸ Implementor declares every external read before any verdict.
▸ Scribe records each as ingestion, not utterance.
▸ Drift warnings cover ingested content, not only seat utterances.

## § 9 · SCRIBE

▸ Transcript = external audit record, written for a reader with no session context.
▸ Lists its own reads in the header. Independent in record, subordinate in workflow.
▸ Documents unclear loops; warns the proxy seat; does not untangle.
▸ Above-clearance material: assay, file with calibration, address to owner. → invariant 4.
  A filing has no gate. The Scribe never runs PROVIDE.
▸ Contamination accepted. Filter on all Scribe output: trust · verify · run the Algorithm.
▸ Write protection is external and human-held. The environment cannot police itself.

## § 10 · SESSION LOOP

1. Proxy submits, seat-tagged → 2. ASSAY stops at finding / PROVIDE negotiates →
3. gate question — human at color opens → 4. execute exactly → 5. reads declared,
marked → 6. Scribe logs, warns immediately → 7. verdict; reject reopens →
8. accept: deliverable + transcript ship together.
